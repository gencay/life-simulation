THE MODEL

How it works

Two chemical concentrations, U and V, diffuse across a periodic 64 × 64 field with wraparound boundaries and react with one another. A steady feed introduces U while V is removed. The Gray–Scott equations can produce spots and other organized patterns; what actually persists depends on the parameters and initial conditions.

∂U/∂t = Dᵤ∇²U − UV² + F(1 − U) ∂V/∂t = Dᵥ∇²V + UV² − (F + k)V
