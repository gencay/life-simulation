THE MODEL

How it works

Two chemical concentrations, U and V, diffuse across a periodic 64 × 64 field and react with one another. A steady feed introduces U while V is removed. That imbalance creates stable spots, waves, and branching structures without a prescribed final shape.

∂U/∂t = Dᵤ∇²U − UV² + F(1 − U) ∂V/∂t = Dᵥ∇²V + UV² − (F + k)V
